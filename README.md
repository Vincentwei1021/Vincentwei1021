## 🎬 video-shotcraft

<a href="https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/video-shotcraft"><img src="assets/card-shotcraft.svg" width="100%" alt="video-shotcraft：让每个产品，都有一次电影般的登场。"></a>

<b>让每个产品，都有一次电影般的登场。</b>agent 走进你的页面，在 124 张镜头配方里为它挑好机位，再让光影、运镜和鼓点替它开口。不借助视频模型，每一帧都由代码写成。<br>
<b>Every product deserves its opening shot.</b> Your agent walks through your pages, picks the angles from 124 shot recipes, then lets light, camera and beat speak for it. No video model: every frame is written in code.

## 🔭 anything2explainer

<a href="https://github.com/Vincentwei1021/anything2explainer"><img src="assets/card-explainer.svg" width="100%" alt="anything2explainer：问出一个问题，点亮一片星空。"></a>

<b>问出一个问题，点亮一片星空。</b>agent 替你翻遍资料，写下讲稿，配上声音，再一镜一镜点亮黑色的画布，直到复杂的事变得清澈。中文英文都可以。<br>
<b>Ask a question. Watch the sky light up.</b> It reads widely, writes the narration, gives it a voice, then lights the black canvas one shot at a time until the complicated turns clear. In Chinese or English.
